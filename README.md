#BMW Dashboard

1 — Project Title / Headline:

🚗 BMW — The Ultimate Sales Machine Dashboard:- 

An interactive and visually rich Power BI dashboard built to explore BMW car sales data — focusing on model performance, fuel type distribution, transmission analysis, price trends over the years, and the relationship between price and mileage.


2 — Short Description / Purpose:

The BMW Sales Dashboard is a visually themed and analytically powerful Power BI report designed to help users explore BMW car data through clean and interactive visuals. Built on a dataset covering 11,000+ BMW cars across multiple models, fuel types, and transmission categories — this dashboard highlights key metrics like total cars, average price, maximum price, average mileage, and top fuel type. This tool is built for automobile enthusiasts, business analysts, sales managers, and Power BI learners who want to understand which models are priced highest, how fuel preferences are distributed, and how BMW prices have changed over the years.


3 — Tech Stack:

* 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used for building all visuals and reports
* 🗂️ Power Query – Used for cleaning and transforming the raw BMW dataset
* 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for KPI cards and custom measures like Total Cars, Avg Price, Max Price, Avg Mileage, and Top Fuel Type
* 📅 Data Modeling – Slicers set up for dynamic cross-filtering across all visuals
* 📁 File Format – .pbit for Power BI template
* 🎨 Theme – BMW inspired dark blue tech theme for a premium visual experience


4 — Data Source:

Source: Kaggle — https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/itszubi/bmw-car-sales-dataset

Dataset contains BMW car records covering 11,000+ unique cars across multiple models, fuel types, transmission categories, and year ranges — including price, mileage, and sales information.

Key Fields:

* Model – Name of the BMW model (X7, 8 Series, M5, etc.)
* Fuel Type – Type of fuel used (Petrol / Diesel)
* Transmission – Gear type (Manual / Automatic / Semi-Auto)
* Price – Listed price of the car
* Mileage – Total mileage of the car
* Year – Year of the car


5 — Features / Highlights:

a — Business Problem:

BMW offers a wide range of car models across different price points, fuel types, and transmission options. Without a clear analytical view, it becomes very difficult to understand which models are selling the most, which fuel type buyers prefer, and how prices have changed over time. Car dealers, sales analysts, and buyers all need a simple and clear way to explore this data and make better decisions.

b — Goal of the Dashboard:

The goal of this BMW Sales Dashboard is to provide one single interactive report where anyone can:

* ✅ See Total Cars, Avg Price, Max Price, Avg Mileage, and Top Fuel Type using KPI Cards
* ✅ Identify the highest priced BMW models using Avg Price by Model Bar Chart
* ✅ Understand fuel type preferences using Sales by Fuel Type Donut Chart
* ✅ Track how BMW prices changed from 2000 to 2020 using Price Trend by Year Line Chart
* ✅ Compare total car count across all models using Total Cars by Model Column Chart
* ✅ Explore transmission type distribution using Sales by Transmission Donut Chart
* ✅ Understand the relationship between price and mileage using Price vs Mileage Scatter Plot
* ✅ Filter the entire dashboard by Year, Model, and Fuel Type using Interactive Slicers


c — Walk Through of Key Visuals:

* KPI Cards (Top Row): Show Total Cars, Avg Price, Max Price, Avg Mileage, and Top Fuel Type — gives an instant snapshot of the entire BMW dataset in one glance.

* Avg Price by Model (Horizontal Bar Chart): Displays the average price for each BMW model — clearly showing that X7 is the most expensive model at 70K, followed by 8 Series at 64K and M5 at 58K.

* Sales by Fuel Type (Donut Chart): Shows how BMW cars are split between Petrol and Diesel — Diesel leads with 7.03K cars vs Petrol at 3.42K, showing strong diesel preference.

* Price Trend by Year (Line Chart): Tracks how BMW car prices have changed from 2000 to 2020 — showing clear pricing patterns and how newer models command higher prices.

* Total Cars by Model (Column Chart): Displays total car count for each BMW model — giving a clear view of which models have the highest inventory or sales volume.

* Sales by Transmission (Donut Chart): Breaks down cars by transmission type — Semi-Auto leads with 4.67K, followed by Automatic at 3.59K and Manual at 2.53K.

* Price vs Mileage (Scatter Plot): Shows the relationship between a car's price and its mileage — helping understand whether higher mileage cars are priced lower.

* Interactive Slicers (3 Filters): Allow users to filter the entire dashboard by Year, Model, and Fuel Type — enabling dynamic cross-filtering across all visuals with just one click.


d — Business Impact & Insights:

* 🚗 Diesel is the most preferred fuel type — with 7.03K cars vs 3.42K Petrol
* 🏆 X7 is the highest priced BMW model — with an average price of 70K
* ⚙️ Semi-Auto transmission is the most popular — leading with 4.67K cars
* 📈 BMW prices have grown significantly from 2000 to 2020 — showing strong brand value growth
* 💰 Maximum price in the dataset reaches 123K — showing BMW's premium positioning
* 📊 Dataset covers 11K+ cars across multiple models — giving a complete market view
